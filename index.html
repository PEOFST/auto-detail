Calendly Bubble Widget

Widget je pripraveny na vlozenie do klientskej stranky jednym script tagom.

Vymen URL domeny a Calendly link za tvoje hodnoty.

<script
  src="https://YOUR-VERCEL-DOMAIN.vercel.app/app.js"
  data-calendly-url="https://calendly.com/dealershipsk/new-meeting"
  data-right="16px"
  data-bottom="16px"
  data-z-index="2147483000"
  defer
></script>
